Cookies and Advertising: What You Need to Know

Privacy & Security November 3, 2026 2 min Published by Vantelyo

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored by your web browser when you visit a website. They can be useful or invasive depending on how they're used.

Types of Cookies

Essential Cookies

These are necessary for a website to work — like remembering you're logged in or keeping items in your shopping cart. Vantelyo uses only essential cookies.

Preference Cookies

Remember your preferences like language, theme, or font size. These improve your experience but aren't essential.

Analytics Cookies

Track how you use a website — which pages you visit, how long you stay. Used by site owners to improve their content.

Advertising Cookies

Track you across multiple websites to show personalized ads. This is the type most people are concerned about.

How AdSense Uses Cookies

When advertising is enabled on a site, Google AdSense may use advertising cookies to show relevant ads based on your browsing behavior. You can opt out of personalized ads at any time through Google Ads Settings.

Vantelyo' Cookie Policy

Vantelyo does not set tracking cookies. We only use essential cookies for site functionality. If advertising is enabled in the future, Google advertising cookies may be set — but you can always opt out.

How to Manage Cookies

In Your Browser:

  • Chrome: Settings → Privacy and security → Cookies
  • Firefox: Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data
  • Safari: Preferences → Privacy

Opt Out of Personalized Ads:

Visit Google Ads Settings to turn off ad personalization.

Take Control

Review your cookie settings today. Opt out of personalized ads. Choose privacy-respecting websites and apps.

Consent is more than a banner. Under rules such as the GDPR, non-essential cookies generally require a clear opt-in, and refusing must be as easy as accepting. Site owners should explain what each category does, remember the choice, and avoid loading advertising scripts before consent. Visitors can also block third-party cookies in their browser or use a privacy-focused extension.

Practical checklist

  • Review permissions once a quarter.
  • Prefer "while using the app" for location access.
  • Revoke camera, microphone, and contacts access when unused.
  • Turn off ad personalization if you prefer non-targeted ads.
  • Keep your device encrypted and back up important data.

Our position

Vantelyo does not require accounts for its apps, does not sell personal data, and designs core features to work offline. Where advertising or analytics could be introduced, we explain it in the privacy policy and ask for consent first.
