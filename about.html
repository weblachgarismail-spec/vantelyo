About Vantelyo

Vantelyo is an independent editorial platform around mobile apps, technology and digital solutions.

We build practical tools to reduce everyday waste — starting with FreshGuard, an anti-food-waste inventory app with expiry reminders, barcode scanner, recipes and savings stats.

Our mission

Help people live more sustainably through simple, privacy-respecting technology. Core features work offline, no account is required, and your data stays on your device.
