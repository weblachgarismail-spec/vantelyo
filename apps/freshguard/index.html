Anti-food-waste inventory with expiry reminders, barcode scanner, recipes and savings stats.

Organize food by fridge, freezer, and pantry. See exactly what you have and where.

Barcode Scanner

Scan products with just-in-time camera permission. Unknown barcodes can be added manually.

🔔

Expiry Reminders

Local notifications fire on your configured dates. Never miss an expiry again.

🍳

Recipes

Recipes matched to what's in stock. Never claims ingredients you don't have.

Savings Stats

Saved = consumed + donated − discarded. Based on prices you enter yourself.

4 Languages

Privacy & Safety

  • 100% local storage — no product photos sent to servers
  • Camera permission only when scanning
  • Notifications only when you enable them
  • Never invents an expiry date from a barcode
  • Colour is never the only status signal — text label always present
  • Never recommends consuming spoiled food
